I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at **Beihang University**, working on medical image analysis and computer vision. Before that, I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from **Liaoning Technical University**.

My research focuses on **multimodal representation learning**, **medical image segmentation**, and **efficient vision models**. I am particularly interested in designing architectures that bring structured priors — shape, topology, and language guidance — into efficient backbones for medical imaging.
